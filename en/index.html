What is Pocknoq

Pocknoq is a fandom companion device that lets you feel your favorite artist's moments — with your body, not just your screen.

A comeback drops, a livestream starts, a new message lands — and instead of one more notification on a screen, a small companion at your side knocks to reach you. Press it and the moment plays as sound, with light and vibration in time. Tap it back, and your reply travels to the artist in return.

👆 Two-way Knock Back 💛 Touch + Light + Sound 🤫 No mic · never listens in ☁️ Curated in the cloud
A The Problem

The moment
slips away

A bond stuck behind a screen, moments buried in a flood of news — and fans pushed outside it.

Meeting your favorite always happens on the far side of a screen. Likes and comments that vanish as you scroll — nothing you can hold, and the sense of being close fades the moment the screen goes dark.

And the news never stops — hundreds of alerts a day. Comebacks, lives, messages — the moments that truly matter get buried in the flood and simply pass by.

And when a moment comes only through a screen and sound, some fans can't receive it at all. So we changed the question — if we redesign how the moment arrives, could no one be left out?

C How it works

How the moment
reaches you

Not hundreds of notifications a day — a few knocks that matter.

A moment arrives

A message your artist sends directly, or an important moment a server picks from the daily noise, arrives as a signal.

It knocks to alert you

The device gives a gentle knock you feel — so you know it's here without looking at a screen.

Press to unfold it

Press it, and the moment plays as sound, with light and vibration moving in time. You choose the senses in the app.

You knock back

Knock Back — your reply gathers and travels to the artist, who can answer the fandom in return.

Tap → knock → answer. Tap your favorite and that knock gathers to the artist, and on the moments they choose, an answer comes back. Every reply voice is really recorded by the artist — the AI only picks which moment to send; it never speaks for them. Not a one-way alert, but a connection you give and get.

Pocknoq is not a conversational AI speaker. It never talks at you or listens in — it delivers the moment, and lets you answer with a knock.
The senses (touch · light · sound), the artists you wait for, the kinds of moments that matter — fans decide all of it in the app.

F One platform, every IP

A device for
every world

Pocknoq isn't a generic gadget — it's born dedicated to each IP.

The server, app, and core hardware stay a shared platform — while the enclosure is 3D printed per IP. Not one shared mold in different colors: the shell itself is made for each artist and character, along with the voice, knock patterns, and app themes. Partners get a device carrying their own world, without building one from scratch.

Shared

Tailored

Fast

Fast rollout

No building from scratch each time — a short cycle turns out a dedicated product per partner.

Why Pocknoq

Six things set it apart — the strengths only this product has.

👆
Two-way

Knock Back, a round trip

Notifications end one-way. Pocknoq comes back — you tap to reply, your reply reaches the artist, and they can answer in return. Not a receive-only device.

💛
Multi-sensory

Three senses, one design

The same moment travels through touch, light, and sound together. Missing one sense doesn't mean missing the moment — Deaf and blind fans belong to the same fandom.

🤫
No Mic

No mic, no listening in

It never talks at you or listens in. There is no always-on microphone — not a noisy smart speaker, but a quiet companion at your side. It still plays sound and voice; there's simply no mic.

☁️
Decision Engine

Server-side decision engine

Hundreds of alerts a day, curated down to the few knocks that matter. The thinking happens in the cloud — so the device stays light and lasts long.

🫳
Engineered Band

Metastructure light band

One band, three jobs — an engineered metastructure that diffuses light softly, carries the knock clearly, and absorbs everyday shocks. This craftsmanship is our core technology.

🏠
Everyday

A place in daily life

It brings the moment of a concert into every day — in your pocket and on your desk. The show ends; the connection doesn't.
