Business
We build and operate our own products: the FAN FOR U fan app and the Pocknoq connected device. We do not provide contract development, agency or consulting services.
Business model
FAN FOR U is a free app monetized through in-app advertising and brand sponsorships. Pocknoq generates revenue from device and cover sales plus a monthly subscription. We work with IP holders under license agreements.
