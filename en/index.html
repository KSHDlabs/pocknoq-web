What is Pocknoq

Pocknoq is a fandom companion device that lets you feel your favorite artist's moments — with your body, not just your screen.

A comeback drops, a livestream starts, a new message lands — and instead of one more notification on a screen, a small companion at your side taps your fingertips. Tap it back, and your knock travels in return. If a light stick belongs to the concert hall, Pocknoq belongs to every day.

👆 Two-way Knock Back 💛 Touch + Light + Sound 🤫 Non-voice · No microphone ☁️ Curated in the cloud
A The Problem

A moment only
some can receive

Fandom's biggest moments arrive through a single sense — and not every fan has that sense.

A comeback announcement. A livestream going live. A new post. Today, fandom delivers these moments through screens and sound alone. For a fan who can't see the screen — or a fan who can't hear — the moment simply passes by. And with hundreds of notifications a day, the ones that truly matter drown in the noise.

So we changed the question — "If we redesign how the moment arrives, could no one be left out?"

"The whole arena turned one color — but someone never saw that light."

A moment that arrived only through sight

"The comeback rang out everywhere — but for someone, it passed in silence."

A moment that arrived only through sound

"It always came one way — and if that way wasn't your sense, it never arrived at all."

C How it works

How the moment
reaches you

Not hundreds of notifications a day — a few knocks that matter.

A moment happens

A comeback announcement, a livestream, a new message — your artist's activity sends a signal.

Only what matters gets through

A cloud-side decision engine picks out only the moments that matter to you — and gets sharper the more you use it.

It arrives as a knock

The moment unfolds as a knock rhythm, light, and sound matched to its kind. You choose the senses in the app.

You knock back

Knock Back — not an alert you only receive, but a reply from your fingertips that travels back.

Pocknoq is not a conversational AI speaker. It never talks at you or listens in — it delivers the moment, and lets you answer with a knock.
The senses (touch · light · sound), the artists you wait for, the kinds of moments that matter — fans decide all of it in the app.

E Only Pocknoq

Why Pocknoq

Six things set it apart — the strengths only this product has.

👆
Two-way

Knock Back, a round trip

Notifications end one-way. Pocknoq comes back — you tap to reply, and the connection travels in both directions. Not a receive-only device.

💛
Multi-sensory

Three senses, one design

The same moment travels through touch, light, and sound together. Missing one sense doesn't mean missing the moment — Deaf and blind fans belong to the same fandom.

🤫
No Mic

Non-voice, no microphone

It never talks at you or listens in. There is no always-on microphone — not a noisy AI speaker, but a quiet companion at your side.

☁️
Decision Engine

Server-side decision engine

Hundreds of alerts a day, curated down to the few knocks that matter. The thinking happens in the cloud — so the device stays light and lasts long.

🫳
Engineered Band

Metastructure light band

One band, three jobs — an engineered metastructure that diffuses light softly, carries the knock clearly, and absorbs everyday shocks. This craftsmanship is our core technology.

🏠
Everyday

A place in daily life

A light stick lives in the concert hall; Pocknoq lives in your pocket and on your desk. The concert ends — the connection doesn't.
