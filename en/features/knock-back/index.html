KNOCK BACK

Tap to send your heart, and the artist picks who to answer

Tap the device to send your heart, like leaving a like → the artist swipes through hearts in their app → and answers the picked fans with a Heartbeat Knock. A 29-second video shows it end to end.

When a knock arrives, press the button within 5 minutes

Screen, sound and vibration tell you a new knock is here. The 5 minutes count from when the artist sent it.

Listening and Knock Backs (sending a heart) are possible within 5 minutes of the knock being sent. There is no replay; after 5 minutes the voice is deleted and only the transcript remains. Heartbeat Knocks only go to fans the artist picks. Screens, artist and voice in the video are examples; the artist app is in development.
